# Offline city catalogue

`src/data/cities.json` is a compact offline fallback for city-name selection before map selection or manual coordinates. It contains populated places with WGS84 latitude and longitude. The catalogue is generated by `scripts/build-city-catalogue.py`; all raw downloads are kept in a temporary directory and are not added to the repository.

## Sources and method

- GeoNames `cities500.zip` supplies populated-place IDs, names, country codes, coordinates and population ranking. The build selects the most populous places in each country, with GeoNames ID as a deterministic tie-breaker. It includes feature codes PPLC, PPLA through PPLA4, PPL and PPLS. PPLX neighborhoods, historical locations and abandoned places are excluded. The source extract's documented scope is places with population above 500 or administrative seats down to level 4.
- GeoNames `alternateNamesV2.zip` supplies Arabic aliases (`ar`) when present. Aliases marked historic are ignored; preferred aliases take precedence. English city names come from the GeoNames primary name field.
- GeoNames `countryInfo.txt` supplies ISO alpha-2 / numeric country identifiers and English country names. Arabic country names are generated from Node.js 22’s CLDR-backed `Intl.DisplayNames` and bundled with the country records; the app needs no DisplayNames polyfill.
- The quota class is derived from Pew Research Center's *Religious Composition by Country, 2010–2020* percentages dataset, published June 9, 2025 and updated February 12, 2026. For each country or territory with a Pew 2020 entry, Muslim share at least 50% means target 70; otherwise target 30. This is only a selection quota classification, not a claim about any individual. Countries/territories with no Pew row are omitted rather than assigned a religion category.

The builder orders eligible places by descending GeoNames population and then ascending GeoNames ID, and takes up to the applicable target. It never repeats a place to fill a quota. Some countries have fewer eligible independent populated places in `cities500` than the quota; `cityCount` reports the actual count and `targetCount` reports the target. At this snapshot, 199 countries/territories are included: 52 receive the Muslim-majority target, 147 the other target, with 7,358 cities total. The 38 shortfalls are: Aruba (10/30), Bahamas (28/30), Bahrain (12/70), Barbados (16/30), Brunei (63/70), Curaçao (11/30), Djibouti (32/70), Equatorial Guinea (28/30), Eritrea (17/70), Eswatini (24/30), Fiji (16/30), French Guiana (20/30), Grenada (10/30), Guadeloupe (25/30), Guam (25/30), Guinea-Bissau (44/70), Guyana (22/30), Kuwait (21/70), Lebanon (51/70), Liberia (27/30), Macao (8/30), Maldives (33/70), Martinique (23/30), Mayotte (64/70), Oman (39/70), Qatar (22/70), Réunion (27/30), Saint Lucia (29/30), Saint Vincent and the Grenadines (21/30), Seychelles (20/30), Solomon Islands (13/30), South Sudan (23/30), Suriname (22/30), Trinidad and Tobago (27/30), U.S. Virgin Islands (8/30), United Arab Emirates (51/70), Vanuatu (10/30), and Western Sahara (26/70). Full machine-readable counts are also in `src/data/countries.json`.

## Regeneration

Run `python scripts/build-city-catalogue.py` with network access. It uses Python’s standard library plus the project’s Node.js 22 runtime for Arabic country labels, writes `src/data/cities.json` and `src/data/countries.json`, and does not retain the source archives. Search and display helpers are exported from `src/domain/cities.ts`.
