# Prayer notifications on Android and iOS

The notification hook schedules the five prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha) for the remaining prayers today and the seven following local Gregorian dates. Sunrise is excluded. It calculates each date with the current coordinates and prayer settings on the device, schedules only future instants, and refreshes the app-owned reminders when the app resumes or when location, prayer method, language, enabled state, each prayer’s voice or sound setting, or phone timezone changes. It never requests location or notification permission by itself. It waits for persisted settings/location hydration (`ready: true`) before reconciling; while foregrounded, it re-arms one timer for each local midnight rather than polling.

Integrate `usePrayerNotifications` from `src/use-prayer-notifications.ts` with the settings state. Pass `ready`, `enabled`, the current coordinates or `null`, prayer settings, language, `prayerSounds` (a per-prayer `{ enabled, voiceId }` map), and translated `labels` (`title`, `channelName`, and a `body(prayer)` function). Optional `testTitle` and `testBody` provide text for the user-triggered sound test. Call `requestPermission()` only in the user's explicit enable action, then persist enabled state. The hook reports `status`, `scheduledCount`, `error`, and `timing`, and exposes `reconcile()`, `openExactAlarmSettings()`, `openAppSettings()`, and `testAlert(prayer)` (Fajr by default). Call `testAlert(prayer)` only from an explicit user action; it schedules one test notification five seconds later using that prayer’s selected voice or silent setting. `status` also reports `channel-muted` when Android has disabled the channel or its sound. Android `timing` is `exact` when the read-only native exact-alarm permission check succeeds, or `may-be-delayed` when Android will use its inexact fallback.

The app registers these config plugins in `app.json`: `./plugins/withPlatformNotificationSounds.cjs` wrapping `expo-notifications`, with 24 files in `assets/adhan/` (12 Android OGG sounds and 12 iOS WAV sounds: four full Adhan choices, Short Adhan and its Fajr variant, a reminder chime and five recorded Salawat phrases) and a white notification icon, and `./plugins/withPrayerExactAlarm.cjs` for `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`. The custom plugin deliberately does not declare `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, which is Play-policy restricted. Expo Notifications adds its reboot receiver and boot permission; scheduled local notifications are restored after device reboot by the library.

Each prayer’s voice resolves to a registered filename from `src/domain/adhan-voices.ts`. Turning a prayer’s sound off uses a silent channel and retains its visual notification; the master toggle cancels every prayer and test reminder. Short Adhan is the fresh default. Saved valid voice/mute choices are preserved. Selecting Short Adhan automatically uses its Fajr variant only for Fajr. Custom recordings must also be registered under `expo-notifications`'s `sounds` plugin option. Android notification channels are immutable after creation, so sound changes use a distinct channel ID. OS notification-channel settings, Do Not Disturb, volume, and vendor battery policies remain in control. A notification sound is played as an Android channel sound; the app cannot promise the entire recording will play. Verify the selected recording on a real device and keep the filename/format consistent with the plugin configuration.

The hook installs a foreground handler on Android and iOS that shows notifications in the banner/list and allows the configured sound. The hook owns only notifications whose `content.data.namespace` equals `quran-adhan-qibla.prayer.v1`; it cancels and replaces those entries without touching other scheduled notifications. The inexact fallback can be delayed by Android, so the UI must not describe timing as guaranteed. The Alarms & reminders action opens Android's exact-alarm access page; the hook rechecks timing when the app resumes. The app notification settings helper opens the notification settings page with an app-details fallback.

## iOS behavior

The same eight-date schedule uses local date triggers on iOS, without Android channels or exact-alarm special access. The hook checks iOS authorization separately from Android’s generic permission status. Authorized, provisional and ephemeral access are recognized; provisional delivery and disabled sound are shown in settings, with a link to the app’s system settings. Permission is requested only when the user enables alerts.

The four full-voice choices use 29-second mono PCM WAV excerpts with an ending fade. Short Adhan is 10.7 seconds, its Fajr variant is 19 seconds, and the separate devotional sounds are shorter still. Apple limits custom notification sounds to less than 30 seconds. The app explicitly labels the short iOS recording. Notification sound settings, volume, Silent Mode and Focus control delivery/sound; full Adhan playback and critical alerts are not implemented. The foreground handler allows the banner/list and configured sound.

Disabling alerts cancels this app’s prayer and test entries. The five-second test replaces an earlier pending test. The displayed scheduled count excludes test alerts. iOS app settings open through `Linking.openSettings()`. No remote push permission or APNs entitlement is used.

## Using the feature

Open the gear sheet, scroll to Adhan alerts, and enable Play Adhan at prayer times. Notification permission is requested at this explicit action. If needed, use the location action to choose a city/device/map/coordinates and on Android the Alarms and reminders action to grant exact-alarm special access. Expand a prayer’s voice row to choose its recording and test that prayer’s sound five seconds later. Choose Short Adhan (the fresh default), Makkah, Al-Madinah, Andrewler or Original. The four recordings use light noise reduction and matching −18 LUFS targets, including separately normalized 29-second iOS excerpts. Invalid/retired voice IDs migrate to Short Adhan without changing mute settings. The licensed temporary short excerpts derive from the Aaqib recording and a Fajr phrase from the Malmö recording; these are not additional full-voice choices. Source processing does not override device volume, Silent Mode, Focus or Do Not Disturb. Per-prayer voice/sound settings and the selected location are restored across launches; no background GPS permission is needed. Open the app at least weekly to replenish scheduled dates. Frequent Salawat reminders share iOS’s pending-notification capacity, so their enabled schedule can shorten this horizon: the app retains the nearest prayers within a 62-request budget and reserves two independent test alerts. At least twelve upcoming prayers fit with the densest supported reminder window. Open the app daily when using frequent reminders. Android retains the normal eight-date horizon.

Turning the setting off cancels pending app-owned prayer/test alerts. Revoked notification access or missing location removes pending app-owned reminders. Clock/timezone changes are reconciled on the next app resume; physical-device reboot, OEM battery policy, DND and complete recording playback require device validation.

If recalculation fails (for example, an unsupported polar location), known previous app-owned alerts are cancelled so the previous city's times cannot continue sounding. Android force-stop disables scheduled work until the app is reopened; launch reconciliation restores reminders from the saved settings and location.
